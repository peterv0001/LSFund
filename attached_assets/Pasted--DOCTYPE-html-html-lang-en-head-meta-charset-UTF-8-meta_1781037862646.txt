      <h2>The rules that keep the brand safe.</h2>
      <p class="intro">In regulated finance, brand and compliance are the same discipline. These are non-negotiable across every surface. (Confirm specifics with counsel — this codifies posture, not legal advice.)</p>
      <div class="guard">
        <div class="grow"><span class="gn">01</span><div class="gt"><h4>It's a purchase, not a loan</h4><p>Describe MCA as the purchase of future receivables. Use "factor rate" (1.15–1.49 multiplier). Never say or imply "APR," "interest rate," or express the factor as a bare percentage.</p></div></div>
        <div class="grow"><span class="gn">02</span><div class="gt"><h4>Every figure is illustrative until verified</h4><p>Calculator outputs and any stat are estimates, labeled as such. Performance metrics (capital deployed, businesses funded, ratings) must be substantiated before they appear — no placeholder numbers ship live.</p></div></div>
        <div class="grow"><span class="gn">03</span><div class="gt"><h4>No income claims on merchant surfaces</h4><p>Earnings figures, agent income, and opportunity language live exclusively on the partner property, behind the appropriate business-opportunity disclosures. They never touch the merchant brand.</p></div></div>
        <div class="grow"><span class="gn">04</span><div class="gt"><h4>Disclosures match the audience</h4><p>Merchant surfaces carry funding-terms and illustrative-figure disclosures only. The anti-pyramid, income, and independent-contractor disclosures belong on the partner site — placing them on the merchant page erodes borrower trust.</p></div></div>
        <div class="grow"><span class="gn">05</span><div class="gt"><h4>State-aware cost presentation</h4><p>Commercial-financing disclosure laws (CA, NY, and others) govern how cost is shown to merchants. Cost presentation follows the strictest applicable standard, not the loosest.</p></div></div>
